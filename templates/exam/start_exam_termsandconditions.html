Rules

  • Sit the exam alone, in one sitting, without help from anyone.
  • Exiting the full screen mode is not allowed.
  • Right-click and copy command is disabled for security reasons.
  • Second devices or switching tabs is not allowed.
  • Taking photographs of the questions or copying questions to external medium is strictly prohibited.
  • This is a single attempt test. Re-test will required another exam booking from your end.

Breaking these rules ends the exam automatically and forfeits the attempt. You can raise a support ticket if you think this was in error, but the exam will not be re-opened and the attempt will not be restored.
