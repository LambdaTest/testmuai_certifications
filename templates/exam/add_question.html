Marks

{% comment %} NEEDS A DECISION. Question.marks defaults to 1, but Exam.MARKS_PER_QUESTION is 5 and Exam.total_marks_for() multiplies by it. If an objective question can be worth anything other than 5, a randomised paper no longer totals count × 5 and the derived maximum marks is wrong. So this shows objective marks as fixed and posts a hidden 5, leaving the input for subjective questions only. The alternative is to let objective marks vary and make total_marks_for() sum real questions — which is the Exam ↔ Question work anyway. {% endcomment %}
