Rebuilt in HTML/CSS from TestMu AI's certificate design (the Playwright 101 sample), with every position and size measured from it. Only the logo, the "Certified Professional" seal and the signature are images, in static/certificates/, all from TestMu AI's Figma file: logo.svg the logo, a vector signature.svg the signature, a vector seal.png cut from Figma's 3× blank-template export (793 px across — sharp in print). An SVG export would be better still. Context — all strings, formatted before they arrive (see the note on dates): holder_name the candidate's name, printed in capitals exam_name e.g. "Playwright 101" — the title (in capitals) and the "for successfully completing …" line issued_on e.g. "October 27, 2026" valid_until e.g. "October 27, 2028" certificate_id the full UUID DATES arrive as text. Django's |date filter converts an aware datetime to the server's zone (UTC) first, which can print the day before or after. SCALE. The design was measured on a 1760 × 1320 canvas. --u is one of those design pixels on the printed page (11in ÷ 1760), so every measurement below is calc(N * var(--u)) and reads the same as the design. The page is 11 × 8.25 in — the design's own 4:3 shape. FONT. Rubik, the closest match to the design (unconfirmed).
